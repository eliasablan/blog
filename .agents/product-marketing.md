**Document version:** v2

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** I build, ship and run web software end to end: code, servers and AI-assisted workflows that let one person deliver like a team.
**Eyebrow / summary label:** Full-stack developer
**What it does:** Elías Ablán is a full-stack developer in Caracas who builds websites and web apps for businesses, then hosts and maintains them on infrastructure he runs himself. He also sets up office IT (networks, Windows Server, Linux). AI is how he works, not what he sells.
**Product category:** Freelance full-stack developer / small-business web and IT partner
**Product type:** Professional services, plus ready-made Solutions he can deploy for a Client
**Business model:** Per-project fee for building, recurring monthly fee for hosting & maintenance. Prices on request, never published.

## Services
1. **Websites & web apps**: sales sites with SEO and a CMS the Client edits, custom apps.
2. **E-commerce**: online stores, including Shopify.
3. **Hosting & maintenance**: deploy, monitor, update and back up the Client's software. The recurring relationship.
4. **IT infrastructure**: office networks, servers, Windows Server domains.
5. **Linux**: servers and workstations, setup and administration.

## Target Audience
**Primary: Clients**: small and medium businesses in Venezuela first, opening to international Clients later, that need a website, store or internal app and have nobody technical in-house. Spanish copy is written for Venezuela first.
**Secondary: Employers**: remote first, open to relocation. Roles in priority order: full-stack developer, AI-adjacent, NetSuite.
**Tertiary: Readers**: developers and tinkerers who come for the Blog.
**Jobs to be done (Clients):**
- Get found and sell online (catalog, quote form, SEO)
- Have one person who builds it *and* keeps it running
- Update their own content without calling a developer

## Brand Voice
| Attribute | Means | Doesn't mean | Example line |
|-----------|-------|--------------|--------------|
| Plain | Short declarative sentences, concrete nouns | Dry or curt | "I build web apps and run the networks and servers they live on." |
| Honest | Admits limits and what he's still learning | Self-deprecating | "I'm still learning a lot of it." |
| Hands-on | Talks about what he did and how | Jargon-dumping | "Notes on what I learn, build, and break." |

**Visual tone:** clean and understated, editorial (IBM Plex Serif/Mono). Never loud.
**Never use (and why):** "10x" (reads as hype), exclamation marks (off-voice).
