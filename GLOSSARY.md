# Elías Ablán — personal site

Personal website and portfolio for Elías Ablán, a full-stack developer. Employers and clients can see his experience and projects and get in touch. Readers can explore his technical writing.

## Language

### Audiences

**Client**:
A person or business that hires Elías to build, host or maintain their software.
_Avoid_: Customer, lead

**Employer**:
A company or recruiter evaluating Elías for a job in development, IT or AI.
_Avoid_: Recruiter, hiring manager

**Reader**:
Someone who comes for the writing. Served by the Blog, never the target of a call to action.
_Avoid_: Visitor, follower

### Content

**Post**:
A piece of writing published on the Blog: a reflection, note or tutorial. There is only one kind.
_Avoid_: Article, essay, note

**Topic**:
One of a fixed set of subject areas that every Post and Project is filed under.
_Avoid_: Tag, category, theme

**Project**:
Any piece of software Elías shows on the site, regardless of whose it is or why it's shown. Every Project is exactly one of the three kinds below.
_Avoid_: Work, piece, portfolio item

**Client work**:
A Project built for a paying Client, presented as proof of what Elías delivers.
_Avoid_: Case study (that's a presentation format, not a kind)

**Solution**:
A Project Elías owns that can be set up, branded, hosted and maintained for a single Client. Not sold as self-serve SaaS.
_Avoid_: Product, SaaS, template

**Side project**:
A Project built to learn or for fun, with nothing for sale.
_Avoid_: Experiment, lab, toy

### Offering

**Service**:
A kind of work a Client can hire Elías for. Priced on request, never listed publicly.
_Avoid_: Package, plan, offer
