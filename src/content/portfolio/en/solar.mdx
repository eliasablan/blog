Solar is a 3D solar system simulator built for the browser. It lets users explore celestial bodies, observe orbits, and control the flow of simulation time.

It also includes an asteroid launcher with configurable radius, density, speed, azimuth, and elevation. The interface combines a space view, camera modes, body census, and control panels.
