I built Autosfront for small businesses in Caracas that sell used cars on consignment. The demo shows the public catalog and the staff dashboard.

## Browsing the catalog

Buyers can search by make or model and narrow the results by price, year, or mileage. Each car has its own page with photos, specifications, and a price in US dollars. From that page, a buyer can ask about the listing or open WhatsApp.

## Working from the dashboard

The staff dashboard shows listings and unanswered inquiries in one place. It also shows which listings are available, hidden, or sold.

The demo uses sample listings and activity. I wrote about the two sides of the app in [Building Autosfront](/en/blog/building-autosfront).
