Manufacturas Adrigal makes galvanized and stainless steel tanks. I built Tanques Australianos so buyers could look up sizes and materials before asking the company for a quote.

## The catalog

Each product page brings together photos, specifications, and a quote form. I built the site with Next.js and connected it to Sanity, where staff can update the catalog. I also set up the sitemap and wrote page titles and descriptions around the products buyers search for.

The company has used the site to sell several products since launch.
