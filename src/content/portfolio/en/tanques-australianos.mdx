## The problem

Manufacturas Adrigal is a steel tank manufacturer in Guacara, Venezuela. It needed a sales website that buyers could find by searching for what they need, and that the company could keep up to date on its own.

## What I built

Tanques Australianos has a catalog, a gallery, technical specifications and a quote request form. It's built with Next.js, Tailwind and Sanity, so the company can update its own catalog. I worked on the SEO as well: sitemap, robots, and page titles and descriptions written around the terms buyers search for.

## The result

Since launch, the company has used the site to sell multiple products.
