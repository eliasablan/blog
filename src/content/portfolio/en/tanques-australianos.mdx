Tanques Australianos is the sales website for Manufacturas Adrigal, a steel tank manufacturer in Guacara, Venezuela. It has a catalog, a gallery, technical specifications and a quote request form.

It's built with Next.js, Tailwind and Sanity, so the company can update its own catalog. I worked on the SEO as well: sitemap, robots, and page titles and descriptions written around the terms buyers search for. Since launch, the company has used the site to sell multiple products.
