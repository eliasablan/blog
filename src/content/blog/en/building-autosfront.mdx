I built [Autosfront](/en/projects/autosfront) as a demo for small used-car consignment businesses in Caracas. I wanted to show both the catalog a buyer can browse and the dashboard a small team can use to handle listings and inquiries.

## From the catalog to a question

The catalog lets you search by make or model and narrow the results by price, year, mileage, or fuel type. On a vehicle page, I put the price and the ways to ask about the car beside the photos. The specifications are below the gallery for someone who wants to check details such as mileage or transmission.

A buyer can ask about a specific listing from that page or open WhatsApp. I kept the listing as the starting point so the conversation begins with a car both sides can identify.

## From a question to the staff dashboard

I put unanswered inquiries near the top of the staff dashboard. A team member opening the panel can see which messages are still waiting and which vehicle each one refers to. The same screen shows recent listings and whether they're available, hidden, or sold.

The demo uses sample listings and conversations. You can browse the catalog, open a vehicle page, and switch to the owner view to see how the team side is organized. [Open the Autosfront demo](https://autosfront.eablan.dev/).
