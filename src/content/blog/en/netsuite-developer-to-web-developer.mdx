From December 2018 to December 2022 I worked as a NetSuite developer at Kavak, in Caracas. Most developers I meet in the web world have never touched an ERP, so this part of my career rarely comes up. It shaped how I work more than I expected.

## What the job was

I wrote and maintained more than 30 SuiteScript automations (Map/Reduce scripts, RESTlets and User Event scripts) used across five or more international markets. I also designed integrations between NetSuite and external web systems, so data moved between them without people copying it by hand.

For the last part of the job I supervised a group of up to seven developers across Turkey and Latin America, reviewing code and managing sprint deliverables while the wider team grew from 6 to more than 25 engineers.
